🛒 E-Commerce Customer Churn Analysis Report

📌 Introduction

Customer churn is one of the most critical challenges faced by e-commerce companies. Churn occurs when customers stop purchasing products or discontinue using online services. High churn rates directly impact company revenue, customer growth, and long-term business sustainability.

This project focuses on analyzing customer behavior in an e-commerce platform and predicting customer churn using data analytics and machine learning techniques. The main objective is to identify the major factors influencing customer attrition and help businesses improve customer retention strategies. 


---

🎯 Objectives

To analyze customer purchasing behavior in e-commerce platforms

To identify the factors responsible for customer churn

To perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

To build machine learning models for churn prediction

To generate business insights for improving customer retention

To help businesses reduce customer loss and increase profitability

📁 Dataset Description

The dataset contains customer-related information collected from an e-commerce company. It includes customer demographics, transaction behavior, app usage, payment methods, and churn status. 

Important Features

Feature	Description

CustomerID	Unique customer identification number
Churn	Customer churn status
Tenure	Duration of customer relationship
PreferredLoginDevice	Device mostly used for login
PreferredPaymentMode	Customer payment method
Gender	Customer gender
CityTier	Tier of customer city
WarehouseToHome	Distance from warehouse to home
HourSpendOnApp	Time spent on app
NumberOfDeviceRegistered	Total registered devices
SatisfactionScore	Customer satisfaction score
CashbackAmount	Cashback received by customer
OrderCount	Number of orders placed

📌 Key Insights

✅ Customers with lower satisfaction scores had higher churn rates.

✅ Customers spending less time on the app were more likely to churn.

✅ High warehouse-to-home distance increased churn probability.

✅ Customers using specific payment methods showed higher churn tendencies.

✅ Customers with low order frequency were more likely to leave the platform.

✅ Cashback and loyalty benefits positively influenced customer retention.
